➕ Addition

Addition means putting two or more numbers together.

Example: 🍎 🍎 + 🍎 = 3 apples
2 + 1 = 3

When we add, the number usually gets bigger.

1 + 2 = 3    |    2 + 3 = 5    |    4 + 1 = 5

➖ Subtraction

Subtraction means taking something away from a group.

Example: 🍎 🍎 🍎 − 🍎 = 🍎 🍎
3 − 1 = 2

When we subtract, the number usually gets smaller.

5 − 2 = 3    |    6 − 1 = 5    |    4 − 3 = 1
